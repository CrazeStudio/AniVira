What AniVira does

AniVira is an anime discovery catalogue. It uses the AniList GraphQL API to display anime metadata such as titles, posters, descriptions, genres, ratings and seasonal listings. Metadata is provided by AniList and may change independently of AniVira.

How video playback works

AniVira does not upload, store, proxy or host anime episodes. The site administrator may add links only to videos they are authorized to share, such as official publisher uploads. Those videos are played using YouTube's embedded player. YouTube and the video publisher control playback availability, regional restrictions, advertising and content removal.

Source responsibility

Adding a YouTube URL does not itself grant rights to the video. The administrator must verify that the publisher has made the video available for embedding and that linking to it is permitted. Unofficial uploads and links intended to bypass copyright restrictions must not be added.

Third-party services

AniList and YouTube are third-party services. AniVira is not affiliated with or endorsed by either service. Their own terms and privacy policies apply when you use their services.
